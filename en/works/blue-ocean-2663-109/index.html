Oh Jiyoon · 오지윤

BLUE OCEAN 2663#109

2026 · 91.0 × 91.0 cm

Mixed media on canvas (24K gold leaf, hanji, natural lacquer)

A great circle floats on a field dark as the night sea, its gold leaf scattered like starlight. The texture ringing its edge draws a quiet boundary.

Series · BLUE OCEAN

Above the sea and beneath it, yesterday and today — the sea changes its voice and colour by the moment, and she reads those changes as the emotions of human life.
