Artist

Oh Jiyoon오지윤

“I watch the motion and the changes of the blue ocean.”— Oh Jiyoon
Artist's official website ↗

A contemporary artist based in Seoul. Through deep colour fields, repeated layers and the symbolic presence of gold, she explores human existence and dignity.

Her rough, layer-upon-layer textures speak of anguish and reflection on life's absurdities, while pure gold, diamond and pearl powder distil a message of human dignity and the light of hope.

BLUE OCEAN

Above the sea and beneath it, yesterday and today — the sea changes its voice and colour by the moment, and she reads those changes as the emotions of human life.

DIGNITY

Deep textures built from repeated strokes speak of life's trials and time; the square that glows within them stands for the dignity inherent in every person.

Now on View

The OH JIYOON : 尊嚴

Dignity — l'essence du souffle

Dates
2 — 30 October 2026
Venue
Hana Bank PLACE 1 · Samseong-dong, Seoul
Works
Some 50 new works
Director
General Director Esther Park

Oh Jiyoon builds a relief of time on the canvas with tens of thousands of strokes. One stroke becomes meditation; their repetition becomes practice.

The layered grain of paint holds life's absurdity and anguish, and the gold set into its cracks becomes hope. Her canvases preach nothing. They simply remain, quietly, and move the heart of whoever stands before them.

Artselah

Artselah is an art agency led by art director Esther Park. She stands beside artists with the eye of a painter who spent thirty years before the canvas, and the hand of a director who has led exhibitions for six.

Not with capital, but with an eye and a voice. So that artists can stand before nothing but their work, we take on exhibition planning, venues, writing and documentation, and connections to sponsors.
